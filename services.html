Dental Implants in Noida

Dental implants are the most permanent solution for missing teeth. A titanium post is placed in the jawbone to support a realistic-looking crown, restoring full chewing function and appearance. Suitable for patients who have lost one or more teeth due to decay, injury or gum disease. Benefits include a natural look, preserved jawbone, and a long-lasting result with proper care.

Endodontics

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment removes infected or inflamed pulp from inside a tooth, eliminating pain and saving the natural tooth from extraction. Required when deep decay or injury reaches the tooth nerve. Performed under local anaesthesia — most patients experience pressure, not pain. Recovery is typically quick, with mild soreness settling within a few days.

Book Consultation
Orthodontics

Braces — Orthodontic Treatment

Braces correct misaligned teeth, overcrowding and bite problems using gradual, controlled pressure. Available in traditional metal and tooth-coloured ceramic options. Suitable for teenagers and adults. Treatment duration varies depending on the complexity of your case — your dentist will explain the full plan at your consultation.

Book Consultation

Smile Designing in Noida

Smile designing is a planned combination of cosmetic dental procedures — including veneers, teeth whitening, bonding and contouring — to improve the shape, colour and symmetry of your smile. Each treatment plan is personalised to your facial structure and aesthetic goals. Ideal for people who want a meaningful and natural-looking improvement in how their smile appears.

Book Consultation
Cosmetic

Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening uses clinically approved bleaching agents to lighten tooth colour by removing stains caused by tea, coffee, tobacco or natural discolouration. Unlike over-the-counter products, in-clinic whitening is controlled, more effective and supervised by a dentist. Results are visible after a single session. Suitable for most adults with healthy teeth and gums.

Dentures

Custom-made full or partial dentures replace missing teeth and restore your ability to eat, speak and smile with confidence. Each denture is fabricated to fit accurately and look natural. Proper fitting ensures comfort in daily use. We provide follow-up appointments to adjust and optimise the fit as needed.

Restorative

Crowns & Bridges

A dental crown is a tooth-shaped cap placed over a damaged tooth to restore its shape, strength and function. A dental bridge uses neighbouring teeth as anchors to replace one or more missing teeth. Both are made from ceramic or porcelain and matched to your natural tooth colour for a seamless result.

Book Consultation
Cosmetic

Veneers

Veneers are ultra-thin porcelain or composite shells bonded to the front surface of teeth to improve colour, shape, size or alignment. A minimally invasive cosmetic treatment, veneers can transform the appearance of chipped, stained, unevenly spaced or slightly misaligned teeth. Results look natural and last for many years with proper care.

Book Consultation
Periodontics

Gum Treatment (Periodontics)

Gum disease affects many adults and often goes undetected until it causes bleeding, recession or bone loss around teeth. Gum treatment — including scaling, root planing and ongoing maintenance — removes bacterial deposits and restores gum health. Early treatment prevents gum disease from progressing and protects the foundation of your teeth long term.

Book Consultation

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry covers a wide range of treatments that improve the visual appearance of your smile — including tooth bonding, enamel contouring, gum reshaping and teeth whitening. Suitable for anyone who wants to address minor imperfections or more significant aesthetic concerns. A consultation will identify the most appropriate and cost-effective options for your specific situation.

Book Consultation
Restorative

Dental Filling

Dental fillings restore teeth damaged by decay, fractures or wear. We use tooth-coloured composite resin that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth — invisible in appearance and durable in function. The procedure is straightforward, quick and painless with local anaesthesia. Regular check-ups help detect cavities early, when filling treatment is simpler and more conservative.

Book Consultation
Preventive

Scaling & Polishing

Scaling removes hardened plaque (tartar) from teeth and below the gumline — deposits that cannot be removed with a toothbrush alone. Polishing smooths the tooth surface and removes surface staining. This professional cleaning prevents gum disease and tooth decay, keeps breath fresh and gives teeth a noticeably cleaner feel. Recommended every six months for most patients.
