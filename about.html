Our Story

From a single chair to a boutique landmark

Trishta Dental Clinic began in 2010 with a single dental chair and an ambitious idea: that a dental visit could feel less like a hospital appointment and more like a calm, considered experience.

Over 15 years, we've grown into a multi-specialty boutique clinic — but our heart hasn't changed. We still believe every patient deserves unhurried time, honest advice and treatment that prioritizes long-term health over quick fixes.

Today, more than 5,000 patients trust us with their smiles. Every one of them is the reason we keep raising the bar on comfort, technology and care.

  • Patient-first, always

    We plan treatment around your comfort, budget and long-term health — never around upselling.

  • Technology that helps

    Every machine we add must make your visit faster, safer or more precise — or it doesn't enter our clinic.

What Drives Us

Mission, vision & philosophy

The principles behind every decision we make and every smile we craft.

Our Mission

To deliver gentle, ethical and technologically advanced dental care that restores not just teeth, but confidence — making every patient feel heard, comfortable and genuinely cared for.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted boutique dental clinic in the country — where modern dentistry meets human warmth, and where visiting the dentist becomes something people look forward to.

Our Philosophy

We treat people, not teeth. Every plan is personal, every procedure is painless, and every interaction is built on honesty, transparency and respect for your time and comfort.
