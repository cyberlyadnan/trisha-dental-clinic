Our Story

A clinic built on trust and care

Trisha Dental Care was established in 2024 with one clear purpose: to offer expert dental treatment in a setting that puts patients first. We believe a dental visit should never feel rushed, impersonal, or intimidating.

Located in Noida (PIN 201301), we serve families, working professionals and children with equal care and attention. Whether you're coming in for a routine cleaning or a complete smile makeover, you'll receive the same level of focus and respect.

We are open every single day — Monday through Sunday — so that access to good dental care is never limited by your schedule. For emergencies, our helpline is always on.

  • Patient-first, always

    We plan every treatment around your comfort, health and concerns — never around upselling or unnecessary procedures.

  • Modern tools, gentle hands

    We invest in equipment that makes your treatment more accurate, less invasive and more comfortable.
